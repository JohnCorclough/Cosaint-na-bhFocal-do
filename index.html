Several words fall at once. Move beneath one, then shoot it with its grammatical gender. The game continues after each hit—there is no Next button. The bubbles are neutral, so they do not give away the answer. Use Z for masculine, X for feminine, and the left/right arrow keys to move. On a phone, drag across the playfield or hold the Move buttons, then tap Masculine or Feminine to shoot. Correct hit: +10 points. Every five consecutive correct hits: +10 bonus. Wrong hit or missed word: −5 points and one life. Scores never fall below zero; empty shots cost nothing. Ten correct hits advance a level, independently of your score. Each new level clears the playfield, gives a short breathing space and restores five lives. Game over offers Retry at your current level; retry restores the score and progress from the start of that level. New game explicitly returns to Level 1. Checkpoints last for this open game session. Press P to pause.
Difficulty and level guide

Beginner starts steadily. Intermediate has 25% faster targets and about 18% more frequent spawns. Advanced has 50% faster targets and about 43% more frequent spawns. All three difficulties progress through the same levels.

Level 1: straight descent. Level 2: sideways drift. Level 3: weaving movement. Level 4 onward: mixed drift and weaving, with faster descent and more frequent spawns. Speed increases through Level 10 and then stays capped for playability. Mistakes do not take away an earned level.
