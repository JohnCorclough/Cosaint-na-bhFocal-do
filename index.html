About these words and their grammatical gender

The new collection contains 59 entries: 36 masculine and 23 feminine. Its source documents overlap; each entry is imported once. Shared words belong to both collections. Masculine and feminine refer to grammar, not the sex of an animal.

Source spellings, meanings and genders are preserved, including regional terms. The original bank supplied genders but no English meanings; translation modes use entries with supplied translations. An em dash means no translation was supplied.

Review notes: “Stail” is supplied as feminine despite its English meaning “stallion”; it has not been changed based on biological sex. “Gadhar” and “Madra”, and “Beithíoch” and “Capall”, have overlapping translations and are treated as valid alternatives. Forms such as “Ruball”, “Trach”, “Faill muice” and plural “Putógaí” are retained for your review, not silently standardised. Original entries such as “ron” and “gréin” are also retained. No dictionary validation is claimed. Automatically generated adjective examples have been removed because the old rule did not account for every phrase or plural.
