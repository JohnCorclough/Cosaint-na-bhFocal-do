How to play & scoring

Move beneath a falling word with the arrow keys, or drag across the playing area on a phone. Fire Z for masculine or X for feminine. The target colours never reveal the answer. Empty shots cost nothing. Press P to pause.

Correct hit +10Five-hit streak +10 bonusWrong or missed −5 & one life

Scores never fall below zero. Every ten correct hits advance a level, clear the screen and restore five lives. Level 2 introduces sideways drift; Level 3 introduces weaving; Level 4 onwards mixes both. Speed and spawn frequency increase through Level 10, then stay capped. Scenery changes at Levels 4 and 7.

After game over, retry your current level with its starting score and five lives. New game returns to Level 1. Checkpoints last for this open page session. Intermediate is 25% faster with about 18% more frequent spawns; Advanced is 50% faster with about 43% more frequent spawns.
